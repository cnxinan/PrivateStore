Attack effect references
=======================

The native C# attack visuals in CombatEffectArt.cs adapt the layered crescent,
directional cross flare, spark spray and broken-ring techniques from pixelfx:
https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx (reference commit a841596)
src/fx/melee/sword-slash.js
src/fx/impacts/hit-spark.js
src/fx/impacts/crit-hit.js

Upstream notice: src/core/sim.js and credited effects adapt Sprite Fusion's
open-source pixel art VFX, Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez, MIT License:
https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx

No pixelfx demonstration actors, act timing or fonts are distributed.

Pixel artwork provenance
========================

The Content/Pixel actors, equipment, NPC, props and most terrain are original
offline coordinate artwork authored for DarkLoot, not Diablo assets.
Editable artwork scripts and provenance are in tools/pixel_art in the source.
Crypt terrain slots 4..11 sample a locally generated stone material, then crop,
remap and redraw it. The saved source records these upstream model labels:

stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0: CreativeML Open RAIL++-M
nerijs/pixel-art-xl: CreativeML OpenRAIL-M per author metadata
madebyollin/sdxl-vae-fp16-fix: MIT

See Content/Pixel/provenance.json for pinned revisions and material edits.
These are source-model license labels, not a blanket license grant for models
or a claim of exclusive copyright over generated pixels. No model weights,
Python packages or generation services are distributed or required at runtime.
